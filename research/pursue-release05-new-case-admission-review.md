## Ranked candidate briefs

### 1. 2021 Gulf of Oman AC-130 EO/IR event — ADD FIRST

**Primary record:** `DOW-UAP-D101`
**Distinct from current Atlas:** No existing Gulf of Oman or AC-130 case.

**What the record supports**

- On **8 September 2021**, a USSOF AC-130 conducted live-fire training over the Gulf of Oman during darkness.
- The Combat Systems Officer used the aircraft EO/IR sensor while orbiting a water-activated flare.
- Two approximately four-foot-diameter **cold** objects were reported stationary 0–20 feet above the water for about 15 seconds.
- During the delay between the 105 mm cannon trigger pull and recoil, the two objects reportedly moved rapidly out of the sensor field without changing altitude.
- The crew reported approximately 25 observations during the sortie, including pairs and groups of three, coordinated altitude changes, and erratic movement.
- TACTOOL-derived estimates were reported as approximately **250–1,300 mph**.
- The complete DVR recording was reported corrupted. A PowerPoint containing **six embedded videos** was listed as an attachment.
- Released coordinates were derived from the AC-130 sensor feed GPS data.

**Page locators:** PDF p. 5 for the event chronology, sensor observations, speed calculation, GPS basis, and DVR loss; PDF p. 6 for the six-video attachment statement.

**Boundary:** This is an intelligence information report explicitly marked **“not finally evaluated intelligence.”** It authenticates the report and sensor-based crew account, not the objects’ identity or origin. DVIDS now assigns PR117–PR122 and six exact DOD filenames to D101. The clips are secondary recordings of an AC-130J infrared display, not native sensor data, and do not independently validate the reported performance.

### 2. 1964 Puerto Rico / USS *Gyatt* radar-visual incidents — ADD FIRST

**Primary records:** `CIA-UAP-D022`, `CIA-UAP-D023`
**Distinct from current Atlas:** The existing Aguadilla case is from 2013 and is unrelated.

**What the records support**

- CIA reviewed reports from **16, 17, 18, 19, and 24 November 1964** near the Atlantic Fleet Weapons Range north of Puerto Rico.
- For 19 November, a Navy F-8C Crusader pilot reportedly distinguished a delta-shaped object about the size of a jet fighter and estimated performance above Mach 3.
- USS *Gyatt* recorded the object on shipboard radar; the memorandum states that radar-scope photographs were taken and describes a track exceeding 300 nautical miles.
- On 24 November, the Atlantic Fleet Weapons Range reportedly held another high-speed, high-altitude target while a separate Crusader at Mach 0.99 and 45,000 feet could not close on it.
- The attached briefing notes state the pilot observed the object in full moonlight with good visibility and that *Gyatt* confirmed tracking with motion pictures of the radar display.
- CIA/OSA requested and received Navy traffic, the **original radar film**, and accompanying prints for scientific analysis.
- CIA stated the object was not a YF-12A or similar Lockheed aircraft.
- A contemporaneous analytical memorandum judged the 19 November object probably a high-performance delta-wing aircraft and discussed a possible MiG-21 reconnaissance interpretation, while expressly noting no evidence that such Cuban or Soviet reconnaissance activity occurred.
- The same memorandum left the other 16–24 November observations classified as unidentified aircraft.

**Page locators:** D022 PDF pp. 3–4 for the incident analysis and competing aircraft hypothesis; PDF p. 6 for original radar-film/print custody and rejection of the YF-12A identification. D023 PDF pp. 1–2 for the concise Navy briefing chronology.

**Boundary:** The original film and prints are referenced but absent from Release 05. The strongest interpretation is a historically important radar-visual intelligence case with an unresolved aircraft identity—not proof of extraordinary origin.

### 3. 2026 red-light/NOD field encounter — ADD FIRST

**Primary records:** `FBI-UAP-D037`, `D040`
**Supporting reconstructions:** `D038`, `D039`, `D041`, `D042`
**Distinct from current Atlas:** The existing `BF-2023-WUS-03` concerns October 2023 narratives. These 2026 FBI records document a different event.

**Why D037 and D040 are one event**

- Both describe the same rainy, mountainous field operation involving two witnesses, night-optical devices, low-level lights, and the same 25-minute mechanical-watch discrepancy.
- D040 functions as corroborating second-witness evidence rather than a separate case.

**What the records support**

- Initial red lights were observed along a western mountain ridgeline in heavy cloud/rain conditions.
- D037’s witness had more than 25 years using NODs and described initial lights for roughly 20 minutes, later lights moving toward the witnesses, one reportedly as low as approximately six feet, and a glow moving through a dry wash.
- D040 describes 6–10 lights synchronizing and traveling east/southeast, with one rapidly descending an estimated 1,000 feet.
- Under closer observation, apparent points resolved into three-light triangular or diamond clusters that moved in tandem without separating.
- At approximately 2350, two lights visible only through NODs reportedly stopped roughly 200–300 meters from the witnesses; another streak/glow moved through a defilade.
- One witness’s mechanical watch became approximately 25 minutes fast while the second witness’s digital watch, phones, and vehicle clock agreed. The second witness confirmed the analog watch had been accurate earlier.
- No physiological effects were reported.

**Page locators:** D037 PDF pp. 1–3; D040 PDF pp. 1–2.

**Boundary:** The event is preserved in two official FBI witness records, but the exact date, geography, identities, and native sensor media are redacted or absent. D038/D039/D041/D042 are reconstructions, not photographs.

**Relationship:** Link as a related Western U.S. red-light/NOD reporting case to `BF-2023-WUS-03`, but explicitly state that the 2026 FBI packet is not the October 2023 event and must not be merged into it.

### 4. 2023 Colorado Springs dark translucent triangle — ADMITTED AS LOWER-CONFIDENCE TESTIMONY

**Primary record:** `FBI-UAP-D026`
**Reconstruction:** `D027`

- Two witnesses reported a fast, silent, low-elevation triangular object over a Colorado Springs residence in October 2023.
- The primary witness described haze/distortion, an almost translucent appearance, a silver-blue trailing band, straight flight, and a rapid bank to near vertical without a corresponding path change.
- Duration was approximately four seconds; estimated altitude and size were subjective.
- The witnesses separated after the event before comparing recollections; the wife independently remembered a dark seam around the edge.

**Page locators:** D026 PDF pp. 1–3.

**Boundary:** Retrospective 2026 FBI interview; no native image or sensor data. D027 is witness reconstruction only. It is distinct from the 2022 Cheyenne Mountain case.

### 5. 2002 Bagram silent triangle — ADMITTED AS LOWER-CONFIDENCE TESTIMONY

**Primary record:** event within `FBI-UAP-D024`
**Reconstruction:** `D025`

- A reservist pilot reported that at about 0430 local in June 2002, stars were obscured by a very large silent triangular form moving west to east over Bagram.
- Estimated speed was approximately 150 knots and estimated size approximately 500 feet, though the witness acknowledged the full shape could not be seen.
- A second pilot beside the witness reportedly observed the same passage and immediately asked, “did you see that?”

**Page locator:** D024 PDF p. 3.

**Boundary:** Reported to the FBI roughly 22 years later; no contemporaneous report, native imagery, or independently interviewed second witness is released. D025 is a reconstruction.

### 6. 2023 Colorado Springs large triangle with red lights — ADMITTED AS LOWER-CONFIDENCE TESTIMONY

**Primary record:** `FBI-UAP-D030`
**Reconstruction:** `D031`

- A witness reported a massive dark equilateral triangle emerging from a distorted/pixelated cloud, with a diffuse red light at each corner.
- The object reportedly accelerated from helicopter-like speed to an estimated Mach 1–2, banked approximately 90 degrees opposite a conventional turn, and produced no sound or visible propulsion.
- The witness reported apparent electronic distortion during a phone call about one minute afterward.

**Page locators:** D030 PDF pp. 1–2.

**Boundary:** Single retrospective witness, subjective size/speed estimates, no native media, and no released technical verification of the alleged phone interference. D031 is a reconstruction. Do not merge with D026 solely because both occurred in Colorado Springs in October 2023.

### 7. 2011 large dark triangle — ADMITTED AS LOWER-CONFIDENCE TESTIMONY

**Primary record:** `FBI-UAP-D028`
**Reconstruction:** `D029`

- Two interviewed witnesses described a large, low, dark triangular object with three recessed diffuse white corner lights.
- Reported duration was 10–15 seconds; estimated dimensions were roughly 200 by 100 feet at about 800 feet altitude.
- The object reportedly moved slowly and steadily and produced a low pulsing hum.
- A third person was reportedly present, but no native video was captured and the location remains redacted.

**Page locators:** D028 PDF pp. 1–2.

**Boundary:** Interviews occurred in 2025, approximately 14 years after the event; no native media or contemporaneous report is released.

### D098–D100 — EXISTING-CASE ENRICHMENT ONLY

#### D098 Utah/Tremonton film analysis — MAJOR EXISTING-CASE ADDITION

`DOW-UAP-D098` is the 4 May 1953 U.S. Naval Photographic Interpretation Center analysis of the **2 July 1952 Utah/Tremonton film** and the 1950 Montana/Great Falls film. It does not establish a separate event because the Atlas already contains `BF-1952-TM-01`, but it materially deepens that dossier.

For the Utah film, the report records:

- a majority analyst opinion that the images were **light sources**, not reflections;
- that the objects could not be identified as natural phenomena or commonly known man-made objects;
- group and individual movement, regular position changes, abrupt directional changes, and objects appearing to remain motionless while others moved;
- conditional size, velocity, and acceleration calculations based on an arbitrary five-mile distance and assumed trajectory geometry;
- the critical custody limitation that the analyzed film was **“a duplicate of a copy”**;
- that the work did not necessarily represent the official Navy/Center position;
- no attempt to corroborate the analysts’ opinions, with the investigation limited by equipment, money, and personnel.

The exact Release 05 source is already mapped to `BF-1952-TM-01` with PDF pp. 6 and 9, alongside the NARA Blue Book file and motion-picture custody record. The computed performance figures must remain conditional because distance and flight geometry were not established.

These records add source depth rather than distinct event cards; D098 is nevertheless one of Release 05’s strongest historical evidence additions.
