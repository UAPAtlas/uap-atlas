# Wave A — Question-Driven Evidence Quality

**Completed:** 2026-08-05
**Cases:** `BF-1957-RB-01`, `BF-1957-NC-01`, `BF-1976-TH-01`, `BF-2019-OM-01`
**Method:** existing local corpus, direct public records and passive public-source checks only. No requests, outreach, submissions, accounts, purchases or fees.

## Purpose

Wave A upgrades the four top-priority objective-reality cases selected from the existing 35-case enrichment backlog. It improves exact source provenance, distinguishes independent evidence families from dependent retellings, removes unsupported narrative compression and states the decisive missing evidence.

## 1. RB-47 ELINT pursuit — `BF-1957-RB-01`

### Primary record

- NARA NAID `28970538`, scans `0396–0399`.
- Four-page 55th Strategic Reconnaissance Wing Joint Message Form.
- Complete 56-object public grouping archived locally.

### Corrected support

The contemporaneous message correlates three reported channels:

1. crew visual observations;
2. airborne ECM direction finding near 2,995–3,000 MHz;
3. ground-radar contact relayed from the Utah ADC site.

### Corrections

- Removed “four independent detection channels.” ECM direction finding is the airborne electronic channel; no separate airborne-radar record is mapped.
- Separated the contemporaneous message from later McDonald, Condon and Blue Book analysis.
- Replaced “single best” language with a source-bounded explanation of why the record is unusually valuable.
- Corrected the structured duration to the contemporaneous message interval, `1010Z–1102Z` on 17 July 1957 (52 minutes); the longer approximately 90-minute duration belongs to later analytical reconstruction.

### Missing decisive evidence

Raw ground-radar plots or film, raw ECM recordings, and the interphone/command-position audio referenced in the message.

## 4. USS Omaha-attributed sphere video — `BF-2019-OM-01`

### Verified custody

Susan Gough's direct email statement, reported by *The Debrief*, confirms that the video was taken by Navy personnel and included in UAPTF examinations.

### Source correction

DVIDS `843593` is a different record. Its official description concerns a July 2019 West Coast night-vision/SLR video of multiple objects later reclassified as unmanned aerial systems. It is not the thermal-style Omaha-attributed clip and has been removed from the case mapping.

### Corrected support

The current corpus supports:

- reported Navy origin;
- reported UAPTF review;
- existence of a public clip/frame showing a dark spot near the horizon fading from view;
- secondary USS Omaha/date attribution from Jeremy Corbell.

It does not establish physical water entry, transmedium behavior, object identity, range, speed, scale, swarm linkage or complete official media custody.

### Missing decisive evidence

Original media master, originating Navy record, embedded metadata, full radar/EW/deck-log packet, sonar record and final analytical disposition.

## Source-family accounting

- **RB-47:** one contemporaneous USAF message family plus dependent later analysis.
- **Northern California:** one complete AIIR packet; internal pages are components, not independent corroborators.
- **Tehran:** one USDAO/JCS message chain relaying several groups; no independent raw sensor family mapped.
- **Omaha:** one named-official confirmation reported by press plus public distribution/mirror context; no complete primary packet.

## Result

Wave A now answers the objective-reality question more cleanly: all four have real, traceable records, but their evidentiary ceilings differ sharply. Record custody, sensor reporting, unresolved interpretation and extraordinary origin are no longer collapsed into one confidence claim.
