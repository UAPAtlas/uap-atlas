# UAP Atlas — Six-Case Source Confidence and Acquisition Pass

**Date:** 2026-07-25

## Scope

Six existing Atlas cases were reviewed without adding cases or sending FOIA, archive, outreach, or private-source requests.

## Acquisition targets

### BF-1961-BH-01 — Betty & Barney Hill

**Recovered:** One primary USAF page: Air Intelligence Information Report 100-1-61, September 21, 1961, preserved by UNH as MC 197, Box 5, Folder 1.

**Atlas change:** Added the upright primary scan, provenance record, exact quote, source record, and university custody URLs.

**Boundary:** The page documents the initial aerial-object report and a possible but indeterminate radar relationship. It does not document an abduction. The referenced controller attachment and fuller Pease/NICAP packet remain unrecovered.

### BF-1975-TW-01 — Travis Walton

**Recovered:** No official packet. A precise contemporaneous press locator was established: Arizona Daily Sun, November 8, 1975.

**Atlas change:** Added the clipping locator and separated it from the still-missing sheriff/search/polygraph packet.

**Boundary:** The locator confirms contemporaneous reporting, not law-enforcement custody or abduction authenticity.

### BF-1987-GB-01 — Gulf Breeze

**Recovered:** No authenticated original. A precise institutional folder was established: Rice University, Richard F. Haines Ufology Papers MS 0706, Series VIII, Box 11, Folder 12.

**Atlas change:** Added the finding aid as an institutional custody lead.

**Boundary:** The finding aid does not identify original Walters Polaroids or establish first-generation chain of custody.

## Internal upgrades

### BF-SF-09 — AAWSAP/AATIP & Skinwalker

Replaced the synthetic summary quote with an exact official AARO statement. Added DIA FOIA contract-status/program-review records and the AARO KONA BLUE history, with separate program-existence and claim-validation boundaries.

### BF-1947-KA-01 — Kenneth Arnold

Upgraded the source to a dated, page-level UPI clipping: Chicago Sun, June 26, 1947, page 2, recovered from Chicago Public Library microfilm with SHA-256 provenance. The record remains contemporaneous press, not independent event evidence.

### BF-1949-SA-01 — San Andres Islands

Replaced generic source tags with page-specific supports and limitations from the February 17, 1949 Headquarters 6th Weather Squadron report, 342-HS1 pages 75 and 77. The record now captures the ship/officer report, measured bearings, wind analysis, rejected/imperfect balloon explanations, and unresolved disposition.

## Result

This pass improved exact custody and source intelligibility without overstating evidence. Hill produced a genuine primary-page recovery; Walton and Gulf Breeze remain acquisition-dependent with substantially sharper locators and boundaries.
