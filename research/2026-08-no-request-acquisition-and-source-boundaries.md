### Calvine — official MoD public-viewer record

Recovered three exact low-resolution TNA public-viewer objects from `DEFE 24/1940`, Discovery `C10130124`:

- viewer p. 112 — handwritten Calvine incident summary;
- viewer p. 113 — 14 September 1990 MoD loose minute;
- viewer p. 114 — defensive press lines.

The selected pages document MoD receipt and review of six negatives, identification of one Harrier and a probable second, no definite conclusion on the large object, and return of the negatives to the *Scottish Daily Record*. They do not authenticate the photographed object or recover the negatives.

The Sheffield Hallam photographic-analysis PDF was also downloaded and retained in durable custody. Calvine remains an acquisition target for the original negatives, remaining reported frames, and fuller photographic-analysis custody.
