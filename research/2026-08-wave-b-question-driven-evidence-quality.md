## Purpose

Wave B applies the existing question-driven evidence standard to four high-leverage records:

- a nuclear-command failure with a disputed UFO association;
- a military multi-night observation whose public legend exceeds the surviving event file;
- a documented official-contact file combined with later disinformation testimony;

The controlling rule is unchanged: an official record proves what that record documents. It does not automatically authenticate every claim relayed inside it, make later testimony contemporaneous, make administrative handling independent event evidence, or establish a non-human cause.

## Case results

### `BF-1967-MA-01` — Malmstrom AFB Missile Shutdown

**Held primary family:** 341st Strategic Missile Wing history and cited engineering material reproduced within an 88-page USAF FOIA packet accessed through The Black Vault.

**Exact event locator:**

- printed unit-history p. 32 / held PDF p. 7: at 0845 on 16 March 1967, all ten Echo Flight sites shut down with Channels 9 and 12 No-Go indications and lost strategic alert nearly simultaneously;
- printed unit-history p. 38 / held PDF p. 14: UFO rumors were reported as disproven; a Mobile Strike Team reported no unusual activity or sightings; the 801st Radar Squadron response was negative for radar or atmospheric-interference problems related to Echo Flight.

**What the packet supports:**

- a real near-simultaneous ten-site strategic-alert loss;
- a substantial engineering investigation;
- a common signal pathway considered as a mechanism, while the reproduced account regarded identified common-mode scenarios as remote;
- contemporaneous negative witness and radar checks concerning the UFO rumor.

**What it does not establish:**

- the exact common cause;
- an object at a launch facility at the shutdown time;
- UFO causation;
- that later object-at-the-gate testimony belongs to the contemporaneous documentary family.

**Correction:** the earlier local locator pointed to `Malmstrom_FOIA_page_005.jpg`, which is the excerpt cover. The event page is `page_007`; the decisive UFO/radar conclusion is newly rendered as `page_014`.

**Source-family boundary:** the 88-page mirror and extracted page images are derivative access to one Air Force documentary family. Later Robert Salas testimony is a separate retrospective testimony family, not independent contemporaneous corroboration.

### `BF-1980-RF-01` — Rendlesham Forest

**Held event record:** Lt Col Charles Halt's 13 January 1981 memorandum.

**Sequence boundary:**

- paragraph 1 relays subordinate security-police reports from approximately 0300 local on 27 December;
- paragraphs 2–3 describe the follow-up and observations in which Halt says he participated on the later night.

**Later record family:** The National Archives (UK) collection guide and 1997 MOD/DEFE parliamentary material document archive custody and later administrative handling. They do not provide a second event-bearing investigation.

**What the record supports:**

- a multi-night sequence involving reported lights;
- a subordinate description of a metallic triangular object;
- later reported observations by Halt's group;
- reported ground depressions and radiation-meter readings;
- a later MOD no-defence-interest position.

**What it does not establish:**

- object identity or origin;
- calibrated radiation significance;
- a radar track;
- a complete event file;
- a confirmed nuclear-weapons nexus.

**Corrections:**

- date normalized to 27–29 December 1980;
- subordinate reporting separated from Halt's firsthand portion;
- UK archive attribution corrected from U.S. NARA to The National Archives (UK);
- `nuclear-facility` removed from the environment classification because the held official record does not establish that nexus;
- DEFE material classified as later administrative context rather than independent event evidence.

### `BF-SF-11` — Bennewitz–Doty

**Held official family:** AFOSI file `8017D93-0/29`, released through a 2 August 2013 FOIA response, including 1980 contact forms and later inquiry material.

**What the official file supports:**

- AFOSI and Air Force contact with Paul Bennewitz;
- official handling and technical review of his reports/material;
- Richard Doty's participation in meetings and communications;
- repeated statements that AFOSI would not investigate the reported phenomena.

**Selected quote:** “SA Richard C. Doty advised Dr. Bennewitz that AFOSI would not become involved in the investigation of these objects.” — AFOSI internal form, 26 November 1980, para. 2.

**Separate source classes:**

1. official AFOSI contact/inquiry records;
2. later public Doty testimony saying he fed disinformation to Bennewitz;
3. the unauthenticated “Project Aquarius” artifact.

**What the mapped evidence does not establish:**

- an official authorization or tasking order for a disinformation campaign;
- the operation's complete chain of command, content, recipients, or scope;
- AFOSI authorship or authentication of the Aquarius artifact;
- the artifact's alien-contact claims.

**Correction:** status changed from a confirmed government disinformation operation to **documented official contact / disputed operation**. Doty's later admission remains relevant attributed testimony, not agency acknowledgment.

### `BF-SF-05` — Majestic 12 Documents

**Held official family:** FBI file `65-81170`, a 24-page FBI Vault PDF documenting received copies, classification inquiries, and advice relayed from AFOSI.

**Exact disposition locator:** held PDF p. 10 relays that the document was bogus and the case should be closed.

**What the FBI file supports:**

- FBI receipt and handling of purported MJ-12 papers;
- classification inquiries;
- a relayed AFOSI bogus determination;
- case-closure advice.

**What it does not establish:**

- authentication of the underlying papers;
- the alleged 1984 mail drop as a verified event;
- the sender, creator, or motive;
- a 1952 MJ-12 program;
- crash retrieval or non-human origin.

**Correction:** the record is now framed as **confirmed FBI custody / no authentication**. “BOGUS” is an agency assessment documented in the file, not proof of who made the papers or why they circulated.
