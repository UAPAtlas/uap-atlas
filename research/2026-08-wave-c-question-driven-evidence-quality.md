## Governing boundaries

1. A broadcast proves that a claimant made a statement; it does not prove the statement true.
2. A real classified site does not authenticate every program claimed to exist nearby.
3. A known atomic number is not a unique prediction of a stable isotope or propulsion property.
4. A patent records disclosure and claims; issuance is not performance validation.
5. A proposal records intended research; it does not establish award, funding, execution, or success.
6. Archival custody authenticates a held proposal copy, not the proposed science or government adoption.
7. A public transcription attributed to an official file remains mirror-qualified until first-party custody is mapped.
8. Demonstration film without calibrated protocol does not establish thrust magnitude, vacuum adequacy, or mechanism.
9. Independent null-result testing constrains a claim but does not prove every apparatus impossible.

## BF-SF-01 — Bob Lazar / S-4 Claims

### Audited conclusion

Lazar's public claim emergence is documented. The alleged S-4 employment, recovered-craft program, stable element-115 fuel, and non-human origin are not independently established by current Atlas holdings.

### Source family 1 — KLAS / 8 News claim provenance

**Direct public source**

- 8 News Now / KLAS retrospective: `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2GRjgBVw9Pk`
- YouTube oEmbed identifies the publisher as `8 News Now — Las Vegas` and the title as *I-Team: A look back at 1989 Bob Lazar interview; it started new UFO conversations*.
- The station identifies the anonymous `Dennis` interview as airing 15 May 1989.

**Supports**

- Lazar publicly made S-4, recovered-craft, gravity-propulsion, and element-115 claims in 1989.
- KLAS is the first-publication channel for the claim family.

**Does not establish**

- Employment or clearance at S-4.
- Existence of S-4 as described.
- Recovered craft, non-human technology, or a stable isotope sample.

### Source family 2 — official Groom Lake history

**Direct public source**

- CIA, *Ask Molly: What really went on at Area 51?*, 1 July 2022: `https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/ask-molly-what-really-went-on-at-area-51/`

**Supports**

- Groom Lake / Area 51 was a genuine secret U-2 and A-12 test and pilot-training site.
- Classified aerospace work and contractor activity occurred there.

**Does not establish**

- Lazar's alleged S-4 facility or employment.
- A recovered-craft reverse-engineering program.
- Non-human origin.

### Source family 3 — official moscovium science

**Direct public sources**

- NIST, *Taking a Seat at the Periodic Table*: `https://www.nist.gov/blogs/taking-measure/taking-seat-periodic-table`
- IUPAC moscovium naming notice: `https://iupac.org/iupac-is-naming-the-four-new-elements-nihonium-moscovium-tennessine-and-oganesson/`

**Supports**

- Element 115 is officially named moscovium, symbol `Mc`.
- NIST describes periodic-table row 7 as all-radioactive and its late heavy elements as laboratory-synthesized.

**Does not establish**

- Lazar possessed a stable isotope of element 115.
- Moscovium has the gravity or fuel properties he claimed.
- Later synthesis validates S-4, recovered craft, or propulsion claims.

**Analytical boundary**

Atomic number 115 followed directly from the periodic table's open sequence. Naming an eventual element at that number was not a unique prediction of a stable isotope, sample, or propulsion mechanism.

### Employment and education boundary

Current Atlas custody does not include an exact LANL directory page, payroll record, badge, clearance, contractor record, MIT/Caltech registrar record, or institutional correspondence.

Public secondary research reports:

- A 1982 *Los Alamos Monitor* article described Lazar as a physicist at the Los Alamos Meson Physics Facility.
- A Los Alamos directory entry reportedly carried a `K/M` contractor notation.
- Public investigators report no located MIT/Caltech attendance or degree evidence.

These remain secondary or locator-only until exact primary pages are mapped. Even authenticated Los Alamos contractor context would not independently establish S-4 access or recovered-craft work.

### Corrected media provenance

Two later videos remain in `video-source-manifest.json` as cultural/contextual material only:

- `d9tdJ2SkBKQ` — Jesse Michels, published 13 April 2026; later claimant interview/profile, not original KLAS custody.
- `Z4_gJ4gJ17A` — Jeremy Corbell, published 16 September 2022; advocacy-oriented context, not independent corroboration.

The prior public-source label that treated the Jesse Michels video as original KLAS context was removed.

### Visual boundary

The locally indexed Groom/Papoose and Area 51 gate images are geographic/editorial context. They do not depict an S-4 interior, Lazar workplace, recovered craft, propulsion hardware, or contemporaneous event evidence.

### Missing decisive evidence

- Primary employment, contractor, payroll, badge, clearance, or program record.
- Primary education records or exact institutional search correspondence.
- Original KLAS broadcast master and full contemporaneous transcript.
- Material sample with documented chain of custody and independent laboratory testing.
- Program documents, hardware records, engineering data, or independent witnesses with separate custody.

## BF-SF-03 — T. Townsend Brown / Project Winterhaven

### Audited conclusion

Brown's patent, Project Winterhaven proposal, defense-interest trail, and laboratory-history context are real. They document claimed apparatus and proposed research, not a validated operational antigravity technology. Independent testing strongly favors an electrohydrodynamic / corona-wind explanation for the observed thrust.

### Source family 1 — inventor patent

**Held primary record**

- `assets/evidence/WINTERHAVEN-1952/WINTERHAVEN-1952-Brown-US1974483-patent.pdf`
- US Patent `1,974,483` / `US1974483A`, *Electrostatic Motor*.
- Filed 7 February 1930; issued 25 September 1934.
- Five pages.
- Local SHA-256: `d0ae542277500162aa03002a6a0982b7978f2ec305b26f0b8a17bcd58ec55cc2`.

**Direct public sources**

- Google Patents: `https://patents.google.com/patent/US1974483A/en`
- Patent image PDF: `https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/c2/71/b6/8e8101a7724b05/US1974483.pdf`

**Exact locator**

- Specification p. 1 / local PDF p. 3: “This invention relates to a method of producing force or motion by applying and maintaining high potential electro-static charges...”
- Claims occupy local PDF pp. 3–5.

**Supports**

- Brown disclosed and patented an electrostatic-motor apparatus.
- The patent contains explicit force/motion claims and technical drawings.

**Does not establish**

- Antigravity or gravity control.
- Useful thrust in vacuum.
- Independent replication, defense adoption, or operational aircraft.

### Source family 2 — Project Winterhaven proposal and archive custody

**Direct public proposal**

- `https://www.thomastownsendbrown.com/hydro/winterhaven.pdf`
- Sixty-six-page image scan.
- Temporary audit-copy SHA-256: `c91de0bae001cd4df0338af1393fe433afa4803b93e15ebf31f578bf2ce2de14`.
- The public PDF was reviewed locally but not added to deployable Atlas assets.

**Archive custody listing**

- University of Maryland Special Collections: `https://archives.lib.umd.edu/repositories/2/archival_objects/187959`
- Cataloged as a 1953 Project Winterhaven proposal in the Oscar Reed, Jr. Papers, Box 20, Folder 6.0.

**Exact proposal locators**

- Proposal p. 1: experiments intended to “confirm or deny” the claimed relationship; expansion depends on results.
- Proposal pp. 17 and 20: controlled vacuum, oil, and reduced-pressure studies are proposed.
- Proposal pp. 21–22: section titled `IMMEDIATE USES IF EXPERIMENTS PROVE TO BE POSITIVE`.
- Proposal p. 27: “No responsibility can be assumed by any of the cooperating institutions to guarantee results in research.”
- Organization-chart issue trail: 20 October 1952 / 1 January 1953.

**Supports**

- A concrete Joint Services-facing research proposal existed.
- The proposal anticipated controlled testing and possible military applications.

**Does not establish**

- Contract award, funding, implementation, completion, or service adoption.
- Successful gravity control, useful vacuum thrust, or operational craft.
- Classified continuation.

### Source family 3 — ONR-attributed evaluation transcription

**Public mirror**

- `http://www.rexresearch.com/ttbrown/ttbrown.htm`
- Attributed to Willoughby M. Cady, ONR File `24-185`, 15 September 1952.

**Reported contents**

- Describes an analytical investigation of Brown's propulsion claims.
- States no allotment was made for an experimental program.
- Attributes documented effects to ionization / electric wind.

**Boundary**

This is a public transcription, not a mapped first-party ONR or NARA scan. It is retained as mirror-qualified evidence of a reported official evaluation, not authenticated official custody or proof that every transcribed phrase is exact.

### Source family 4 — independent technical test

**Direct public source**

- M. Tajmar, *The Biefeld-Brown Effect: Misinterpretation of Corona Wind Phenomena*.
- Public PDF: `https://tu-dresden.de/ing/maschinenwesen/ilr/rfs/ressourcen/dateien/forschung/folder-2007-08-21-5231434330/ag_raumfahrtantriebe/Biefeld-Brown-Effect-AIAA-Journal-Revised.pdf?lang=en`
- Twenty pages.
- SHA-256: `3f86ed016f61b661df1911d93417a2995021ffdb43196fc8da9a45fde13f6d45`.

**Exact locators**

- Abstract, PDF p. 1: no thrust detected within experimental accuracy; anomalous force constrained far below corona wind.
- Discussion, approximately PDF pp. 10–11: no noticeable linear thrust; upper limit below `10 µN` for the tested configuration.
- Conclusion, approximately PDF p. 12: no linear thrust observed; corona-wind effects were misinterpreted as gravitational coupling.

**Supports**

- A directly inspected independent experiment tested a non-corona interpretation.
- The experiment produced a null result within its measurement limits.
- Conventional electrohydrodynamic explanation is strongly supported for the tested configuration.

**Does not establish**

- That every Brown apparatus is impossible.
- That no electrohydrodynamic propulsion application exists.
- That historical defense interest did not occur.

It does directly constrain the extraordinary interpretation and practical propellantless-propulsion claim.

### Missing decisive evidence

- First-party custody for Project Winterhaven and ONR File 24-185.
- Contract-award, funding, implementation, completion, or adoption records.
- First-generation Bahnson film custody and calibrated test notebooks.
- Neutral vacuum replication with full pressure, voltage, current, thrust, outgassing, and uncertainty data.
- Evidence of operational craft or classified successful continuation.
