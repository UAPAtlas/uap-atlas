## Classifier correction

Operational triage now treats structured, unresolved `acquisitionTargets` as authoritative. Recovered/duplicate bookkeeping rows do not keep a case open. Evidence-limit prose alone does not create an acquisition task. Quote-confidence classification evaluates the declared leading tier, avoiding false positives where a high-confidence quotation is followed by lower-confidence interpretation language.

## Boundaries

- Publication, archive, or institutional custody authenticates the record layer—not the reported object, entity, performance, or origin.
- Public mirrors do not substitute for original media, native metadata, or first-party custody.
- Locator-only records are not treated as inspected contents.
- No new-case admission or Blackfile confidence increase is part of this tranche.
