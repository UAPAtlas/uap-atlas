## What it supports

- The Hills reported a strange aerial object seen through their windshield while traveling south on Route 3 near Lincoln, New Hampshire, during the night of September 19-20, 1961.
- Pease AFB combat intelligence collected and signed a report on September 21, 1961.
- The report records a separate radar incident and states that time and distance could hint at a possible relationship.

## What it does not establish

- It documents an aerial-object report, not an abduction.
- It explicitly says the radar observation provided no description and that no relationship could be determined.
- The referenced `Daily Report of Controller` attachment is not included in the recovered page.
- The complete Pease/Blue Book/NICAP packet and later hypnosis source record remain outside current mapped custody.
